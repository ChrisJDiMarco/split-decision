# Motion capture credits

Motion clips in `assets-src/mocap/cmu/` come from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database,
mocap.cs.cmu.edu (BVH conversions via github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap). The database was
created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217. The data is free to use in products, including
commercial ones; the raw data itself may not be resold.
